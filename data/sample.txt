1. **Integration of RAG Architecture**:
   - The bot combines the GPT LLM with company-specific data sources, such as database records and files, to provide context-rich responses.
   - The use of RAG ensures that responses are grounded in facts derived from the company’s data.

2. **Database and File Indexing**:
   - Jasiri meticulously structured the company's data sources, including customer, product, transaction, and payment information, into a format that the LLM can query seamlessly.
   - This allows the bot to provide answers that are both precise and actionable.

3. **Personalized Assistant Approach**:
   - To enhance user experience, the bot is designed to speak conversationally, like a friendly yet professional assistant.
   - This approach ensures that employees feel supported when navigating complex business metrics.

4. **Focus on Transparency**:

5. **Debugging and Adaptability**:
   - Jasiri implemented robust debugging tools to monitor and refine the bot’s performance.
   - This iterative development process ensures the bot remains adaptable to new data and evolving business needs.
